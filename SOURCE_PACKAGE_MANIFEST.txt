Paper Moon startup implementation:
- native splash held at module load;
- Paper Moon begins from the first root Stack frames, independently of auth redirects;
- asset file + image decode + layout gate, with bounded layout, decode and splash-handoff fallbacks;
- independent anchored thread;
- Reanimated UI-thread movement;
- real auth content remains mounted under the overlay.

Required handoff files are enforced by scripts/verify-source-zip.sh.
Forbidden dependency/build/cache directories are excluded by scripts/package-source-zip.sh.
The resulting APK must pass scripts/verify-apk.sh and Android device QA before release.
